What is a Neuron?

Neurons are specialised cells that carry messages around the body. They allow you to think, move, feel and react.

Fast Facts

  • Signals travel as electrical impulses.
  • The brain contains about 86 billion neurons.
  • Some nerve signals can travel over 100 m/s.
